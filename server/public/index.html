NDVI Crop Monitoring

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index

NDVI measures vegetation health from NIR and Red light reflectance. It helps identify crop condition, stress areas, and supports data-driven management decisions.

NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red) Range: -1 to +1

NDVI Health Indicator

Very Healthy0.6 – 1.0 · Dense, healthy vegetation
Healthy0.3 – 0.6 · Good vegetation condition
Moderate0.1 – 0.3 · Stress or uneven growth
Poor-0.1 – 0.1 · High stress, sparse vegetation
Very Poor-1 – -0.1 · Bare soil / very poor condition
